About TaxWise India

TaxWise India is an independent online resource designed to help individuals understand and compare estimated income tax under India's old and new tax regimes.

Our Purpose

Our goal is to make basic income tax calculations easier to understand. The calculator is intended to help users explore how income, eligible deductions, and other inputs may affect their estimated tax liability under different tax regimes.

Our Approach

We aim to present information in a simple, accessible format. We also aim to improve our calculator and educational content as applicable tax rules change.

Accuracy and Limitations

Tax calculations depend on the relevant financial year, applicable legislation, income type, eligible deductions, rebates, surcharges, cess, and individual circumstances. Our calculator provides estimates and may not cover every tax situation.

Please verify results against official government information or consult a qualified tax professional before making financial or tax-related decisions.
